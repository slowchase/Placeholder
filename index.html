about

placeholder is a weekly field note for finding something worth leaving the house for.

it is a small edit of exhibitions, openings, performances, food, music, and odd things happening around san francisco — made for curious people who want a useful list without feeds, algorithms, spam, or fluff.

contact

send events, ideas, corrections, local tips, collaborations, questions, or a simple hello to events@placeholdersf.com.

faq

what is placeholder?
